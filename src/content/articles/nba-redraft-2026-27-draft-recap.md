**Correction (2026-10-15):** This note was rewritten on the day it was first filed. Teams are now named as Team Name (First Name), and the analysis adds sourced NBA context. The draft board itself is unchanged.

The NBA Redraft board is locked: 12 teams, 13 rounds, 156 picks, zero games played. That last number matters. Nobody has won anything. Nobody has lost anything. What exists is a permanent public receipt of what twelve managers chose to do with a snake draft, and the desk has brought a highlighter, a magnifying glass and zero mercy.

## The top five, audited

Ben's Best Team (Ben) opened with Nikola Jokić at No. 1, and there is nothing to roast. Jokić averaged 27.7 points, 12.9 rebounds and 10.7 assists in 2025-26 and finished second in MVP voting. A Jokić triple-double average as the safe pick. The desk is annoyed it has to say "correct."

Then there is the running Shai Gilgeous-Alexander free-throw joke. Before anyone tells it, the desk checked the stat sheets. Gilgeous-Alexander made 540 of 614 free throws in 2025-26 and won his second straight MVP. Luka Dončić attempted 645. The joke has the wrong address. The bigger free-throw frequent flyer of the two went second overall to PP (Aaron), who took Luka Dončić with the No. 2 pick and then added Jalen Brunson at No. 23, presumably so the possession never has to end.

Which leaves Isaac's Impressive Team (Isaac) holding Shai Gilgeous-Alexander at No. 4. Fourth. Gilgeous-Alexander, the back-to-back MVP who averaged 31.1 points last season, slid past three managers like a lost receipt in a parking garage. Isaac's Impressive Team (Isaac) gets the steal of the top five, and three teams get to explain to the rest of the league, slowly and with visual aids, why Gilgeous-Alexander was not their guy.

Jet's Supreme Team (Jet) took Giannis Antetokounmpo at No. 5 and bought the most honest offense in basketball: run, jump, dunk, repeat. Giannis Antetokounmpo made 15 of 45 threes in 2025-26, so the jumper exists the way a spare key exists. Antetokounmpo still shot 62.4 percent from the field on 373 of 598, because why would anyone stop running at the rim when the rim keeps saying yes. The only place that plan stalls: Giannis Antetokounmpo made 232 of 357 free throws, 65.0 percent, the one spot on the floor where nobody lets him run first.

## Team names, checked against the roster

A team called lawemby (Tianna) drafted Victor Wembanyama at No. 3. If that name was a prophecy, it cashed. Wembanyama was the first unanimous Defensive Player of the Year in 2025-26, led the league with 197 blocks and finished third in MVP voting. Two managers looked at that and picked someone else first. Both picks were defensible. The desk still wants it noted.

Three picks later, cade and em (Jeremy) took Cade Cunningham at No. 6. Cunningham averaged 23.9 points and 9.9 assists in 2025-26 and made the All-NBA First Team. At minimum, the team name now contains the correct spelling of its first pick, which is more than most fantasy team names manage.

Then there is team (Zachary). A fantasy team named, and the desk cannot stress this enough, team. It took Tyrese Maxey at No. 11 and LeBron James at No. 50, and the most memorable decision on the roster is still the name. Twelve teams got a blank name field. One of them typed the label back into it.

## Roster construction, or something like it

The best value on the board belongs to justin's Scary Team (Justin), which took Kon Knueppel at No. 69 in round 6. Kon Knueppel made 273 threes in 2025-26, the most in the NBA, and finished second for Rookie of the Year. The NBA's leading three-point maker went 69th. Every team in this league, his eventual one included, walked past him for five full rounds to get to the snacks.

That stings most for Michael's Monstrous Team (Michael), which spent the No. 27 pick on LaMelo Ball. LaMelo Ball made 272 threes in 2025-26, one fewer than Knueppel. So Michael's Monstrous Team (Michael) paid a third-round price for second place, and justin's Scary Team (Justin) got first place in round 6. In fairness, Michael's Monstrous Team (Michael) took Cooper Flagg at No. 10, and Cooper Flagg won 2025-26 Rookie of the Year with 21.0 points per game, so the desk will allow one good decision. Then came Jamal Murray at No. 15, Dejounte Murray at No. 51 and Keegan Murray at No. 118. Three Murrays. That is not a roster, that is a subscription.

Tyler's Top Team (Tyler) waited until No. 139 to take a point guard, Jrue Holiday. In a draft that took 42 of them. That is not contrarian. That is a group project where everyone assumed somebody else was bringing the ball.

Sesames Life Gamble (Mohamed) turned the third-round reversal into three of the first 25 picks: Karl-Anthony Towns, then Scottie Barnes one pick later, then Evan Mobley at No. 25. The gamble in the team name has a specific address, and it is the free-throw line: Evan Mobley made 180 of 297 free throws in 2025-26, 60.6 percent.

Matthew's Magnificent Team (Matthew) finished with four centers and four point guards, a roster built like it heard the word "balance" once and took it personally.

And the final pick, No. 156, went to Ben's Best Team (Ben): Dillon Brooks, who averaged 20.2 points in 2025-26. Pick 156. Brooks, a 20-point scorer last season, was the final selection of the entire draft. That says less about Ben's Best Team (Ben) than about the 155 picks that went before him, several of which the desk would like to see audited.

## The desk's read

Interpretation, labeled as such. Best draft-day decision: justin's Scary Team (Justin) getting Kon Knueppel in round 6. Best value at the top: Isaac's Impressive Team (Isaac) with Gilgeous-Alexander, the reigning MVP, at No. 4. Best team-name follow-through: lawemby (Tianna) and cade and em (Jeremy), tied. Most likely to be asked who runs the offense: Tyler's Top Team (Tyler). Most disrespectful pass of the draft: the three teams that let Gilgeous-Alexander, a back-to-back MVP, slide to No. 4.

None of this is a result. It is twelve sets of decisions, on the board and in public. Once games are played, the desk gets real numbers to be rude about.
