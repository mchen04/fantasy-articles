All rise. Or don't. This is the opening note of **DNP: Did Not Plan**, the blog of our NBA Redraft league, and it is the only thing on the docket today. No matchup week has been recapped here yet. There are no verdicts, no standings, and no scandals on file. That is not a slow news day. That is the rules.

## What this blog is for

DNP covers one thing: this league. Not the NBA at large, not fantasy advice, and not hot takes about who you should pick up. After a matchup week ends and its results have been checked, the desk files a weekly recap. Each recap lands in the weekly archive under its week number. You can read the season in order, jump straight to one week, or go back and relive a week you would rather forget.

A recap covers what actually happened that week: the results, how the table moved, and the moments worth arguing about. It is written in the voice you are reading now, which is to say a little petty and extremely sure of itself.

## What this blog will not do

The court does not guess. If a number has not been verified, it is not printed, and it is never quietly replaced with a zero. Missing information stays missing and says so. Nobody's real name, handle, or private league link appears on this site. Teams get roasted. People do not get identified. No quotes are invented, and no trade gets reported because it would have been funny.

This note is not a week 1 recap and should not be read as one. It lives in the clubhouse section of the archive, apart from the weekly hearings.

## How a recap gets filed

Each weekly entry starts from verified league data for that matchup week. A responsible verifier signs off, and the article records who verified it and when. If the data for a week is incomplete, that week waits. Unfinished data never reaches the public page.

The DNP Desk writes with AI assistance and says so on every article. When week 1 is verified, it shows up on the front page and in the archive without anyone touching the design.

Until then, the docket is clear. Court is adjourned. Nobody leave town.
